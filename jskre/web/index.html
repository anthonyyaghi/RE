Market by town

A wide gap between the 25th and 75th percentile $/m² means tired and finished stock trade far apart in that town, which is the precondition a flip needs. Towns with many renovation targets and many finished comps are the best hunting grounds — a wide spread on three listings is noise, not a signal.

Price cuts and delistings

A seller who has cut once will usually cut again, so reductions are the strongest motivated-seller signal in this data. A delisting is often a sale — those are what will eventually let you calibrate the model against real exit prices instead of asking prices.
